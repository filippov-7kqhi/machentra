About us

Four machines chosen for tight entry, with every width published

MachEntra is a UK online retailer of compact machines for places big plant cannot reach. The range is four machines — a mini excavator, a 500 kg tracked dumper, a petrol wood chipper and a stump grinder — each chosen for how narrow it is.

We do not manufacture and we do not warehouse. Machines are crated at our supplier's warehouse overseas and delivered free to the UK mainland in 7–12 working days. The checkout price is final: nothing more is due when the machine arrives.

Each one carries a 30-day return, two years on parts and one year on the engine, and our phones are answered Monday to Friday, 8am to 6pm.
